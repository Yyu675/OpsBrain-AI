## 二、V11 落器（确证级，两枚）

| 索 | 对口查询 | 缺口证据 | 来源 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `idx_alert_group_dedup (service, module, last_occurred_at DESC) WHERE status IN (FIRING,ACK) AND ticket_id NOT NULL` | `findActiveGroupTicket` 风暴聚合退路 | dedup 未命中时每条告警必跑；现有 service 索引挂 first_occurred_at,窗口列不对口 | AlertRepository(方向 E) |
| `idx_ticket_create_time (create_time DESC)` | `countCreatedToday` / `countCreatedByDay` | 首页 KPI 每次加载必扫;create_time 列素无对口索引 | DevOpsTicketRepository |

**锁窗纪律**:V11 不用 CONCURRENTLY(Flyway 事务内禁）；随表规模增长，
后续索引铸型一律走「CONCURRENTLY 双段迁移」（先并发出索引，再版本化标志）。
